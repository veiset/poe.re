# Data usage

For the generated data on https://data.poe.re and its copies in this repo. The
[AGPL](LICENSE) covers the code, not the data.

## Fine without asking

- Testing, and your own tools and addons
- Sharing with friends
- Small, free tools, up to about 1,000 users

## Needs permission

For larger tools and tools that make money, ask on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/T8BzKnatY6) or in an
[issue](https://github.com/veiset/poe.re/issues).

## Not allowed

Using the data for a copy of poe.re or poe2.re.

## When you use it

- Credit poe.re with a link.
- Send a User-Agent: `<name>/<version> (<url>)`.
- Cache the files.

The data can change or go away without notice.
